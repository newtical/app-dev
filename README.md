## My Favorite Movie: The Hunger Games 

*The Hunger Games* is a **science fiction adventure film** based on the novel by *Suzanne Collins*.  
It takes place in a dystopian future where the powerful **Capitol** forces the **12 districts** of *Panem* to send a boy and a girl to fight to the death in a televised event known as *The Hunger Games*.

### Main Themes
- **Survival** – Competing in a deadly arena where only one can live  
- **Rebellion** – Standing up against oppression and injustice  
- **Sacrifice** – Risking one’s life for loved ones  
- **Media Manipulation** – How entertainment and fear are used to control people

### Why I Love It?
I love the cinematography of the movie. It feels so ethereal and adventurous at the same time. Also, I like how the actors played their characters so well, especially Peeta and Katniss. I just love the thrill of the movie all throughout.
